Learn Ecommerce Metrics with Phoebe · course 1 of the ecommerce & growth analytics series

Every ecommerce number, explained on one store

A leader track that teaches you to read a store's numbers without computing anything - what conversion rate actually means (and why two tools report two different ones for the same shop), where the funnel leaks money, how to run the weekly review, and which questions expose a shaky metric. And an analyst track that computes everything on one running store: Mango Lane, a mobile-first fashion app with 240,000 monthly visitors, 360,000 sessions, 9,720 orders, and $660,960 GMV - decomposed completely across eight sessions. The centerpiece is a live driver-tree simulator: edit any lever (PDP penetration, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, AOV) and GMV recomputes instantly; simulate a drop and trace the coral trail back to the guilty gate. No install, no account, no bill.
